# Rawaj SaaS: Project Overview & Landing Page Content

## 1. Project Description: What is Rawaj SaaS?
Rawaj is an AI-powered SaaS platform tailored specifically for e-commerce businesses in the Gulf region (running on Shopify, Salla, or Zid). It is designed to act as an all-in-one growth and customer management engine. 

**What it does:**
* **AI Product Content Engine:** Automatically generates high-quality, conversion-optimized product titles, short descriptions, and SEO metadata in authentic Gulf Arabic dialect and English.
* **Unified Social Inbox:** Consolidates messages from WhatsApp Business, Instagram DMs, and Facebook Messenger into a single dashboard. It automatically scores leads and provides AI-generated reply drafts.
* **AI Lead Pipeline:** Tracks customers comprehensively from the first message to their final paid order on a visual Kanban board, alerting store owners of stale deals.
* **Smart Order Hub:** Syncs directly with Shopify, tracks revenue, and uses AI to send automated WhatsApp recovery messages for abandoned carts.

## 2. Landing Page Scenario & Flow
**The Target Audience:** Gulf e-commerce merchants looking to save time on product listing, customer service, and increasing sales conversions.
**The Journey (Scenario):** 
1. The merchant arrives on the page and immediately realizes this tool speaks their language (literally and figuratively) because it focuses on Gulf e-commerce.
2. They scroll to see the core features to understand that this replaces multiple tools (copywriter, inbox tool, CRM).
3. They look at a step-by-step "How it works" section to see how easy it is to integrate with their store (Shopify/Salla/Zid).
4. They review the pricing to find an affordable entry point (checking QAR/USD pricing).
5. They click the main Call to Action (CTA) to start their free plan.
